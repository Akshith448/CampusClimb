### Tuned Threshold (cosine >= 0.75) — 60-pair held-out eval split:

| Method | Set | F1 | Precision | Recall | 95% Wilson CI |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Baseline (cosine >= 0.75) | Full (60 pairs) | 0.7013 | 0.5400 | 1.0000 | [0.5763, 0.8021] |
| Baseline (cosine >= 0.75) | Hard (40 pairs) | 0.5965 | 0.4250 | 1.0000 | [0.4426, 0.7335] |
| Jaccard Fix (+ jaccard >= 0.25) | Full (60 pairs) | 0.6897 | 0.6452 | 0.7407 | — |
| Jaccard Fix (+ jaccard >= 0.25) | Hard (40 pairs) | 0.5263 | 0.4762 | 0.5882 | — |

Paper headline for dedup: F1 = 0.7013 (full set) / 0.5965 (hard-case) at threshold 0.75.
Baseline at 0.85 gives F1 = 0.5625 (lower due to low recall).
Jaccard fix adopted: cosine-only threshold 0.75 is the final method.
